# Product Pivot

## Previous Product

The repository originally contained CareerOS, a career and job-search platform.

The project was subsequently developed as OpsOS, an intelligent technical operations
system.

Those implementations remain valuable engineering history.

## New Product Direction

The product is now being pivoted toward intelligent real-world planning.

The problem:

People frequently have to combine information from many disconnected sources to
answer a simple question:

> "What should I do, and how do I make it happen?"

For example, planning an outing can require:

- maps
- transport schedules
- ride prices
- restaurant menus
- restaurant prices
- event information
- opening hours
- social media
- weather
- budgets
- group preferences

The user currently has to assemble these pieces manually.

The new product aims to perform that assembly.

## Product Hypothesis

People do not necessarily need another place to search.

They need a system that understands their situation and turns fragmented information
into a practical decision and plan.

## Initial Product Scope

The first implementation should focus on:

1. Intent capture
2. User context
3. Constraints
4. Places / activities
5. Plans
6. Plan items
7. Budget calculations
8. Decision reasoning
9. Saved plans
10. Plan modification

Real-time external information and broader integrations can follow once the core
planning model is stable.

## Explicit Non-Goals

The product is not initially:

- a generic chatbot
- a simple restaurant directory
- a travel booking clone
- a social network
- a maps replacement
- a generic recommendation feed

The central product is an intelligent planning engine.
